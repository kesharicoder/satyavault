# AI Governance & Safety Standard — Satya Vault

## 1. Principles
1. **Advisory Role Only**: AI tools are strictly advisory. No legal determination or judicial decision is automated.
2. **Permission-Filtered Grounding**: RAG vector queries MUST enforce case-level access control before retrieving context.
3. **No Direct Data Leakage**: Document contents are never sent to external providers unless explicitly configured and authorized.

## 2. Refusal & Disclaimer Policy
Every AI output MUST carry the standard disclaimer:
> *"Advisory Notice: This response is generated by an automated intelligence model using source-grounded case records. It is intended strictly for administrative assistance and does not constitute a legal opinion or judicial finding."*
